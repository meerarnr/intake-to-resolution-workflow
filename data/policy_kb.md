# Support Policy Knowledge Base (mock SOPs)

## Billing
- Refunds up to the manager-approval threshold may be processed by a support agent after verifying the charge.
- Refunds above the threshold require manager approval before any money moves.
- Never promise a refund date; quote the standard 5-7 business day window.

## Technical
- Check `known_resolutions.json` first. If a known fix applies, share the steps.
- Escalate to Tier 2 if the issue persists after the known fix or no fix matches.
- Never ask users for passwords or full API keys.

## Account
- Verify identity (email on file + one security question) before any account change.
- Ownership transfers and deletions always need human approval.
- Lockouts: send the reset link; do not disable MFA without approval.

## General
- Acknowledge within the SLA window for the ticket's urgency.
- Any mention of legal action, regulators or attorneys goes to a human immediately.
- VIP customers get a named human owner; keep replies concise and apologetic where warranted.
